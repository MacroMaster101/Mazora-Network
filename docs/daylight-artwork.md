## Prompt set

Shared direction: Relight the referenced dark Mazora scene into a polished daylight counterpart. Preserve camera, landmark geometry, aspect ratio and composition. Use finely crafted voxel stonework, natural limestone, muted evergreen foliage, clean water, restrained amethyst accents and diffuse skylight. Keep the center calm for website copy and the edges detailed. Avoid blown highlights, hard black shadows, artificial white overlays, neon purple fog, vignettes, blur, text, logos, watermarks, people or UI panels.

### valley-daylight-v3

Reference: `public/images/mazora-world-continuation-bg.webp`.

Wide 16:9 valley backdrop used behind website headings and dashboards. Lock the left foreground three-arch bridge, central lake and waterfalls, right terraces, mountain silhouettes and floating crystals in the SAME locations. Soft natural morning haze should make the distant middle and upper landscape gently pale with low visual contrast, while side and bottom landmarks remain readable. Keep the original composition rather than adding an empty sky.

### home-hero-daylight-v3

Reference: `public/images/worlds/home-hero-dark.webp`.

Wide 16:9 homepage hero. Lock every floating island, the left and right bridges, central distant portal, waterfalls, trees, terraces and village silhouette exactly where they are. Central sky should be quiet pale blue-lilac with subtle soft clouds; the lower edge should blend naturally into a misty bright valley. Preserve the dark version's frame and view.

### store-hero-daylight-v3

Reference: `public/images/worlds/store-hero-dark.webp`.

Wide 16:9 marketplace hero. Preserve the two towering village walls, left market awnings, right crystalline outcrop, distant valley and very large empty central sky in exactly the same places. Make central sky pale blue with a faint soft cloud veil, suitable behind dark typography. Keep all architecture and camera position.

### vote-hero-daylight-v3

Reference: `public/images/worlds/vote-hero-dark.webp`.

Extra wide 5:2 sanctuary hero. Preserve the symmetrical left/right stone temples and stairs, two tall amethyst-tipped columns at the upper corners, distant circular portal, centered crystal plinth, proportions and camera. Calm pale blue-grey daylight sky with a soft atmospheric gradient. No strong sky texture or bright sun behind the centered website title.

Refinement pass: Preserve all geometry and framing. Use soft overcast skylight, a pale powder-blue sky, high-key ivory and silver-grey central stonework, shallow shadows and delicate natural atmospheric mist. Reduce central contrast for dark website typography. Keep the portal and crystal pastel amethyst, the temples detailed, and water reflections light. No flat panel, white blob, blur, glow, added sunbeam, clipping, text or UI.

### news-hero-daylight-v3
